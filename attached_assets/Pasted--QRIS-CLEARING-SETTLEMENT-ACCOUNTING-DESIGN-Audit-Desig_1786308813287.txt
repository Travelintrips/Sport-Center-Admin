# 3. DEFINE ACCOUNTING EVENTS

Pisahkan dengan tegas:

## Event A — QRIS PAYMENT CONFIRMED

Economic event:

Customer sudah membayar, tetapi dana masih berada pada provider/acquirer dan belum masuk rekening bank CST.

Expected accounting:

DEBIT:
`QRIS Clearing / Receivable from Payment Provider`

CREDIT:
`Revenue`

CREDIT:
`PPN Output`

Contoh payment Rp100.000 dengan PPN:

DEBIT  QRIS Clearing      Rp100.000

CREDIT Revenue            Rp90.090

CREDIT PPN Output         Rp9.910

Bank Mandiri CST TIDAK boleh didebit pada event ini untuk transaksi baru setelah cutover.

---

# 4. EVENT B — QRIS SETTLEMENT RECEIVED

Saat actual bank mutation terbukti merupakan settlement provider:

Contoh:

Gross settlement:
`Rp100.000`

MDR:
`Rp300`

Net bank credit:
`Rp99.700`

Expected accounting:

DEBIT:
`Bank Mandiri CST` Rp99.700

DEBIT:
`MDR Expense` Rp300

CREDIT:
`QRIS Clearing` Rp100.000

Settlement entry tidak boleh mengakui revenue atau PPN lagi.

Revenue/PPN sudah diakui pada payment confirmation.

---

# 5. NO DOUBLE REVENUE / TAX

Hard invariant:

Settlement MUST NOT create:

* revenue;
* PPN output;
* sales tax transaction kedua.

Settlement hanya memindahkan balance:

`QRIS Clearing`

→

`Bank + MDR Expense`

Tax remains linked to original payment accounting event.

---

# 6. CLEARING ACCOUNT DESIGN

Audit COA existing terlebih dahulu.

Cari apakah sudah ada account yang secara semantik cocok:

* QRIS Clearing
* Payment Gateway Clearing
* Settlement Receivable
* Receivable from Payment Provider
* Merchant Receivable
* Electronic Payment Clearing

Jangan membuat COA baru jika account valid sudah tersedia.

Jika tidak tersedia, PROPOSE account baru terlebih dahulu.

Preferred classification:

Asset / Current Asset

Suggested semantic:

`QRIS Clearing / Payment Provider Receivable`

Jangan memilih nomor COA secara sembarang.

Ikuti numbering convention CST yang existing.

Jika COA baru membutuhkan business/accounting approval:

STOP sebelum membuatnya dan return:

`CLEARING_ACCOUNT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`

---

# 7. PROVIDER DIMENSION

Clearing harus dapat diaudit per provider:

* `mandiri_direct`
* `paylabs`

Tentukan apakah:

A. satu GL clearing account dengan provider metadata/subledger;

atau

B. clearing account berbeda per provider.

Gunakan desain existing accounting/subledger terlebih dahulu.

Jangan membuat provider-specific COA tanpa alasan accounting yang kuat.

Minimum requirement:

setiap settlement harus dapat ditelusuri ke provider.

---

# 8. PAYMENT ACCOUNTING CUTOVER

Untuk transaksi QRIS BARU setelah cutover:

jangan debit:

`Bank Mandiri CST`

pada payment confirmation.

Debit:

`QRIS Clearing`

instead.

Non-QRIS flow tidak boleh berubah.

Pastikan:

* cash;
* bank transfer;
* non-QRIS payment;

tetap memakai behavior existing kecuali memang secara eksplisit termasuk scope.

---

# 10. SETTLEMENT ELIGIBILITY

Settlement accounting hanya boleh dibuat jika strict QRIS matcher menghasilkan deterministic match.

Required hard boundaries:

* company
* provider
* canonical bank account
* expected settlement/business date
* payment partition
* gross amount
* net bank credit
* MDR/deduction
* provenance

Unknown provider:

`REVIEW`

Ambiguous partition:

`REVIEW`

Wrong bank account:

no settlement posting.

Date mismatch:

`REVIEW`

MDR mismatch:

`REVIEW`

Negative deduction:

`REVIEW`

Tidak boleh accounting-post settlement yang masih REVIEW.

---

# 11. MDR CALCULATION

Do not hardcode:

`0.3%`

atau:

`0.7%`

sebagai production truth.

MDR harus berasal dari effective-dated approved provider configuration.

Required dimensions minimal:

* company
* provider
* effective_from
* effective_until
* MDR/rate
* rounding policy
* settlement rule/version

Jika approved configuration tidak tersedia:

`MDR_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED`

dan settlement posting tidak boleh dilakukan.

---

# 12. MDR ROUNDING

Gunakan aturan rounding yang eksplisit.

Audit apakah provider melakukan:

* rounding per transaction;
* rounding per settlement batch;
* atau rule lain.

Jangan mengasumsikan.

Store/audit:

* gross
* expected MDR
* observed deduction
* net settlement
* rounding variance
* effective rate

Tolerance harus configuration-driven.

---

# 13. SETTLEMENT JOURNAL BUSINESS KEY

Settlement journal wajib idempotent.

Define canonical business key.

Preferred basis:

company

* provider
* bank mutation ID
* settlement partition/reference

Contoh correlation semantic:

`qris_settlement_{mutation_id}_{partition}`

Tetapi ikuti naming convention project existing jika sudah ada.

Database/application harus mencegah settlement mutation yang sama menghasilkan journal dua kali.

---

# 14. PAYMENT → CLEARING SUBLEDGER

Untuk multi-payment settlement, settlement record harus menyimpan hubungan ke seluruh payment IDs.

Example:

Settlement mutation X

→ payment 101
→ payment 102
→ payment 103

Store deterministic partition/linkage.

Jangan hanya menyimpan total gross tanpa payment-level linkage.

* source payment ID

* company ID

Settlement accounting minimum metadata:

* company ID

* bank mutation ID
* bank account ID
* settlement reference
* payment IDs/partition
* gross
* net credit
* MDR

* settlement date
* source/provenance
* correlation/business key

Do not overload immutable ledger identity fields if live schema protects them.

Use additive metadata/linkage where necessary.

# 16. INTERNAL SPORT CENTER JOURNAL

DEBIT QRIS Clearing

CREDIT Revenue

`Bank Mandiri`

while public accounting says:

`QRIS Clearing`

Internal/public accounting semantics should agree.

# 17. MIRROR BEHAVIOR

`public.sport_payments` remains payment mirror.

It should carry canonical dimensions including:

* source_payment_id
* company_id
* payment_method
* payment_provider
* provider references
* canonical bank/settlement account
* paid_at
* expected settlement date
* payment accounting entry ID

Do NOT treat mirror `bank_account_id` as proof that bank settlement already occurred.

Distinguish:

`expected/canonical settlement account`

from:

`actual settlement received`.

If current schema conflates them, document the issue and propose additive field rather than silently changing semantics.

# 18. BANK MUTATION PROVENANCE

Settlement accounting requires actual bank evidence.

Mutation must preserve:

* source_app
* source_module
* source table/import
* source ID
* import batch ID
* sanitized raw payload/reference
* source classification

Required classification:

`actual_bank_mutation`

Synthetic/forecast mutation cannot trigger accounting posting.

# 19. PURE SETTLEMENT ACCOUNTING EVALUATOR

* matched payment partition;
* provider;
* company;
* gross;
* bank credit;
* MDR config;
* bank account;

* settlement date.

* debit bank

* credit clearing
* correlation key
* status
* reason

Pure evaluator MUST NOT write DB.

---

# 20. ACCOUNTING INVARIANTS

For each proposed settlement:

`gross = bank_credit + MDR`

subject only to approved rounding tolerance.

Accounting:

`total debit = total credit`

and:

`bank debit = actual mutation credit`

`MDR debit = observed/approved deduction`

`clearing credit = matched payment gross`

If invariant fails:

STOP posting.

Return:

`SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNTING_INVARIANT_FAILED`

---

# 21. CLEARING BALANCE INVARIANT

For a fully settled payment partition:

Payment confirmation:

DEBIT clearing = gross

Settlement:

CREDIT clearing = same gross

Net clearing impact:

`0`

unless legitimate partial/unsettled balance remains.

Provide audit showing clearing opening, debit, credit, and residual.
