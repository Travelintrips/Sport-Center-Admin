# SPORT CENTER — PHASE SC-FINANCE-BOUNDARY
## Payment Confirmed → Finance Event → STOP

PROJECT:
Sport Center

OBJECTIVE:

Refactor Sport Center so its financial responsibility ends at:

Business transaction / booking
→ payment
→ payment gateway / proof
→ payment confirmed
→ durable finance event/outbox
→ STOP

Everything AFTER the finance event will later be owned by Supabase Central Finance.

IMPORTANT:

This phase is ONLY for Sport Center boundary preparation.

DO NOT build the final Central Finance processor yet.
DO NOT modify BizPortal yet.
DO NOT migrate Tenant yet.
DO NOT republish production unless explicitly instructed.
DO NOT perform uncontrolled production data cleanup.

Existing Paylabs/payment gateway behavior MUST remain operational.

============================================================
ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT
============================================================

SPORT CENTER OWNS:

- booking
- booking groups
- membership/payment business flow
- payment creation
- payment amount
- payment type
- payment method selected by customer/admin
- payment proof
- Paylabs checkout/request
- Paylabs callback/webhook verification
- provider transaction references
- payment confirmation
- refund/cancellation BUSINESS state where applicable
- canonical sport_center.sport_payments
- durable finance event creation

SPORT CENTER STOPS AFTER:

sport_center.sport_payments.status = confirmed
→ finance event/outbox successfully recorded

SPORT CENTER MUST NOT OWN AFTER CUTOVER:

- public.sport_payments mirror creation/update
- public.accounting_payments creation
- public.accounting_entries creation
- public.accounting_entry_lines creation
- accounting journal posting
- final PPN calculation/posting
- COA selection
- ledger posting
- MDR accounting
- settlement accounting
- bank reconciliation posting

These responsibilities will move to Central Finance in a later phase.
