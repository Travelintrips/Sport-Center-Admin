# VERY IMPORTANT CURRENT STATE

Architecture baru yang sudah ada di Supabase adalah:

```text
Payment confirmed
        ↓
sport_center.accounting_journals
status = draft
        ↓
approve
        ↓
posted
```

QRIS payment journal menggunakan:

```text
DR PAYMENT_CLEARING
   CR SPORT_REVENUE
   CR PPN_OUTPUT
```

PPN sudah selesai pada payment journal.

Jadi audit settlement harus menganggap:

> Settlement TIDAK menghitung revenue atau PPN lagi.

Settlement hanya menangani konsep:

```text
Posted Payment Clearing
        ↓
Settlement Batch
        ↓
Gross
        ↓
MDR / Provider Fee
        ↓
Net
        ↓
Bank
```
